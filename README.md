# Hospital Information System (HIS)

## Overview

This Hospital Information System (HIS) is designed to streamline and manage various processes within a hospital setting. The system includes modules for In-Patient Department (IPD), Out-Patient Department (OPD), Billing, and Appointment scheduling.

## Features

### In-Patient Department (IPD)

- **Admission:** Easily admit patients to the hospital.
- **Ward Management:** Manage and allocate beds in different wards.
- **Patient Records:** Maintain detailed records of in-patients.

### Out-Patient Department (OPD)

- **Appointment Scheduling:** Allow patients to schedule appointments with doctors.
- **Prescription Management:** Generate and manage prescriptions for out-patients.
- **Medical History:** Maintain a comprehensive medical history for each patient.

### Billing

- **Invoice Generation:** Automatically generate invoices for services provided.
- **Payment Tracking:** Keep track of payments and outstanding balances.
- **Billing Reports:** Generate reports for financial analysis.

### Appointment Module

- **Online Booking:** Enable patients to book appointments online.
- **Doctor Availability:** Display doctors' schedules and availability.
- **Notification System:** Send reminders and notifications for upcoming appointments.
